Stockfish (engine/stockfish-19-lite-single.js and engine/stockfish-19-lite-single.wasm)

The chess engine is Stockfish 19 in its WebAssembly build, Stockfish.js 19.0.1 by Nathan Rugg and
Chess.com (https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js), the "lite, single-threaded" flavour, unmodified,
as published in the `stockfish` package on npm. The network it uses is by Chris Bao (sscg13).

Stockfish is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3: the full text is in
engine/COPYING.txt. Its source code:

  Stockfish.js (the build used here):  https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js/tree/v19.0.1
  Stockfish itself:                    https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish

The engine runs as a separate program (a web worker); the trainer talks to it only by sending and
receiving text in the UCI protocol. The trainer's own code is under the MIT licence (LICENSE); the two
files named above stay under the GPL.
