The opening names and their moves (data/book.js, data/openings.js) come from the Lichess chess-openings
data set, https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings, which is in the public domain (CC0 1.0,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The evaluations beside them were computed for
this tool with Stockfish.
